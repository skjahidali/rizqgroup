Generative Engine Optimisation · GEO

Give people and systems a clearer picture of your business.

Generative search experiences draw on many changing systems, sources and signals. GEO is not a switch that guarantees a brand mention. We focus on fundamentals you can improve: accurate information, genuinely useful expertise and consistent references to your business.

What GEO work can include

  • Reviewing how your business, services and locations are described across your website and public profiles.
  • Improving priority pages with clear facts, useful explanations and visible authorship where appropriate.
  • Finding gaps or inconsistencies in important sources and business details.
  • Planning original content that answers meaningful customer questions.
  • Checking technical access and structured information on your own website.
  • Tracking relevant changes over time without promising AI citations or recommendations.

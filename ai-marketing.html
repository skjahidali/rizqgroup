AI Marketing & Automations

Start with a real task—not a tool looking for a problem.

We look for repetitive steps that can be supported by automation, then design a workflow with clear inputs, human review and a sensible fallback. AI can assist with first drafts or sorting information, but your team supplies context, checks accuracy and approves what goes out.

What AI marketing work can include

  • Mapping a current research, content or lead-handling process.
  • Identifying low-risk, repeatable steps where assistance may save time.
  • Designing human checkpoints, ownership and clear approval rules.
  • Choosing tools that fit the workflow and considering data-handling requirements.
  • Testing outputs for accuracy, usefulness and brand voice before wider use.
  • Reviewing the workflow with your team and improving it from real feedback.

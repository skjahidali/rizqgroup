Good to know

Questions worth
asking.

Not sure where to start? Here are a few useful things to know.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO improves your visibility in traditional search results. AEO helps your expertise appear in direct answers. GEO focuses on how your brand and content are understood and referenced in generative search experiences. A strong strategy considers how they work together.

Do I need to do SEO and AI search optimisation separately?

They have different considerations, but share important foundations: useful content, a well-structured website and clear evidence of expertise. We assess your goals and current visibility before recommending a practical combination.

How do we know if the work is making a difference?

We agree on relevant measures with you at the start—such as organic visibility, qualified enquiries or engagement with key pages—and review progress against those goals. Results vary by market and starting point, so we don't promise one-size-fits-all outcomes.

Can you manage Google or Meta ad campaigns?

Yes. We can help plan and manage paid campaigns, including audience and campaign setup, landing-page alignment, conversion tracking and ongoing optimisation. Advertising spend is separate from management fees, and campaign results depend on your market, offer and budget.

Can you work with our existing team or agency?

Yes. We can collaborate with in-house teams and other partners, clarify responsibilities and focus our work where it adds the most value.

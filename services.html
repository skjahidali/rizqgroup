Before we begin

Good to know.

We’ll help you focus on what makes sense for your business today.

How do I choose a digital marketing agency in Kolkata?

Look for a clear understanding of your market, a practical scope of work, transparent measurement and honest expectations. Ask how the agency approaches SEO and backlinks, what it will report and what depends on your team or budget. Be cautious of guaranteed rankings or shortcuts. We agree priorities and measures before recommending ongoing work.

Do you work with businesses across India and worldwide?

Yes. RizqOne is based in Kolkata, India, and partners with businesses across India and around the world. We shape the service scope around your audience, market and goals.

Do you offer SEO, AEO and GEO as one package?

We can coordinate the work, but the right scope depends on your website, market and goals. We’ll discuss priorities before recommending a plan.

Can you guarantee rankings, AI recommendations or ad results?

No. Search platforms and AI systems make their own decisions, and advertising results vary by market, offer and budget. We focus on sound practices, clear communication and transparent progress rather than guaranteed outcomes.

Do backlinks still matter for SEO?

Relevant links that people and industry publications choose to make can help people discover your work and give context about your business. We focus on earning mentions through useful resources, genuine partnerships and PR—not buying or automating links to manipulate rankings.

Is ad spend included in campaign management?

No. Advertising spend is paid to the platforms and is separate from our campaign planning and management fees. We’ll discuss budget and scope with you before work begins.

Can we start with a specific service?

Yes. We can begin with the need you already have, then suggest adjacent opportunities only if they’re relevant.
